    TV mode

    Panels only, without the menu and the top buttons, for a screen in the control room. Press Esc to leave.

    Polling

    how often every device is read
    Every 10 minutes at the most. Every reading is saved in the database the moment it is read. For a one-off read at any time use "Read devices now" at the top. The setting is kept in data\settings.json and survives restarts.

    Reading the devices

    how often each one is asked
    The master controller gives the live view: who powers the plant, the gensets, the mains and the solar. Reading it less often means less load on it, and the live view moves less often. Every other device is read at the site read, or less often to give it more rest. Turkey's feed is built at the site read either way; the values of a device read less often than the site read show as stale to Turkey in between. Applied at once, kept in settings.json after restarts and updates.

    Updates

    new versions from GitHub
    The updater looks for a newer release every 4 hours. With automatic updates on, it installs it at night (01:00–05:00). The settings and the history stay. Installing stops the collector for about a minute, Turkey's feed included. If the new version does not start, the updater puts the previous one back. The buttons work only in the window on the PC the collector runs on.
